## Review collection order
1. Ask the most satisfied recent clients first
2. Prioritize projects with clear scope and credible outcomes
3. Request reviews that mention the business problem solved, not generic praise
4. Favor branded or natural-anchor review text over keyword stuffing

## Backlink targets for the first 90 days
### Month 1
- Complete Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, LinkedIn, and GBP profiles
- Publish founder and company summary posts on LinkedIn
- Secure 10 to 15 branded directory and profile mentions

### Month 2
- Guest posts or founder interviews on startup, ecommerce, and web-industry sites
- Partner mentions from collaborators or clients where appropriate
- Case-study syndication or summary mentions on relevant communities

### Month 3
- Outreach for agency roundups and "best web development agency" lists where editorially defensible
- Local India citations and startup community mentions
- Unlinked brand mention campaign focused on Devacia plus founder identity

## Anchor text policy
- Majority branded anchors: Devacia, Devacia studio, Devacia website
- Secondary natural anchors: Devacia website development, Devacia custom software
- Use partial-match service anchors sparingly
- Avoid exact-match spam anchors in bulk

## Do not do
- Fake reviews
- PBN links
- Low-quality paid directory spam
- Bulk forum/comment links
- Fabricated awards or rankings
- Unsupported client/result claims

## AI visibility reminders
- Keep founder, company, and service descriptions identical across profiles
- Link back to the main service hub, website development page, custom software page, and strongest case studies
- Prefer editorial mentions and founder commentary over mass-produced link placements
