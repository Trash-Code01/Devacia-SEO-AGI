## First four concrete briefs

### B2B wholesale quote system (C01)

- **Destination:** proposed `/solutions/b2b-wholesale-quote-system`, parent `/services/custom-software-development`.
- **Buyer:** wholesale owner/operations lead handling catalog inquiries and quotations manually.
- **Promise:** explain whether a website-connected RFQ workflow fits their operation.
- **Sections:** concise answer; catalog→RFQ→qualification→quote→follow-up diagram; role permissions; editing/admin scope; exceptions and approvals; integration boundaries; when ordinary ecommerce or off-the-shelf software is enough; actual project example; scope and recurring costs; buyer FAQs.
- **Evidence:** confirm Yoglivya Wholesale scope independently from the rebrand story; review link plus permissioned screenshots; no invented time/revenue savings.
- **CTA:** “Map your quote workflow”; fields ask business type, current process, catalog size and bottleneck, with only necessary contact data.
- **Links/schema:** software and automation parents, relevant case, method guide, Service/provider Organization, breadcrumbs.
- **Success:** qualified RFQ-system inquiries and opportunity quality; early measures are eligible indexing and relevant impressions.

### WordPress service refresh (K01)

- **Destination:** existing `/services/wordpress-development`; observed enterprise query already ranks on average 6.6 from 25 impressions.
- **Sections:** who should use WordPress; actual content model/editor experience; hosting/update responsibilities; integration and migration process; redirect preservation; scope table; support boundary; relevant project walkthrough; estimate process.
- **Title candidate:** “WordPress Development Services for Business Websites | Devacia”. Validate intent and live SERP before changing; do not stuff “enterprise” without supporting scope/capability.
- **Proof:** permitted WordPress build examples and actual maintenance practices. Clearly distinguish enterprise-ready process aspirations from demonstrated enterprise customers.
- **CTA:** discuss rebuild/editing requirements. Evaluate query-specific CTR with enough subsequent impressions and leads, not a short-term ranking promise.

### Ecommerce rescue evidence upgrade (C02)

- **Destination:** `/solutions/ecommerce-conversion-rescue`, linked from ecommerce/CRO services and the canonical diagnostic article.
- **Sections:** traffic quality versus site friction; funnel stages; mobile product/cart/checkout observation; diagnose before rebuild; what data is needed; scope of audit versus implementation; platform limitations; actual case.
- **Evidence:** matched periods, traffic mix, sample denominators and simultaneous changes if reporting conversion effects. Otherwise report implemented improvements only.
- **CTA:** request diagnostic; offer a short plain-language checklist before requesting detailed store access. Do not request credentials through the public form.

### Quote journey benchmark (R01)

- **Destination:** proposed `/research/b2b-quote-journey-benchmark-2027` with accessible methodology and data download if releasable.
- **Sections:** research question; sampling frame; rubric; findings with denominator; missing data; examples with fair context; limitations; reproducibility; author/reviewer; date; practical checklist.
- **Source-worthy distinction:** original structured observations plus operator insight, not a compilation of third-party statistics.
- **Distribution:** specific figures for industry editors, a founder explanation, operator walkthrough, methodology contribution and qualified workshop invitation. No generic mass press-release blast.

## Governance and refresh policy

Owner-editor logs primary intent, source, author/reviewer, evidence permission, canonical URL, related service, CTA, publication date and next review. The founder approves factual claims about client work. Engineering validates schema, links and rendering. Analytics checks outcome semantics and source fields.

Every quarterly review compares content cost, relevant search demand, assisted inquiries and won-opportunity evidence. Missing business data leads to better instrumentation or research, not fabricated attribution. A tool earns publication by helping a buyer make a decision; a study earns publication through defensible observations; a case earns publication through actual work. This is the content advantage competitors cannot reproduce merely by rewriting the same generic article.
