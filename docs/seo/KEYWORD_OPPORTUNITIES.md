## Scalable discovery pattern, not scalable doorway publishing

Use **Industry × Problem × System × Location × Intent** to generate interview/search candidates:

- Wholesale × missed inquiries × quote portal × India × development company.
- Restaurant × phone booking clashes × reservation workflow × Prayagraj × website with online booking.
- Fashion D2C × weak mobile checkout × CRO diagnostic × India × agency comparison.
- Manufacturer × unqualified inquiries × RFQ landing page × Delhi × redesign service.

Do not generate every permutation. Validate language through sales conversations, support tickets, real briefs, Search Console, public community questions and representative localized SERPs. Record whether a buyer needs a service page, answer within an existing page, a case study, or a tool. Research demand without inventing a volume figure.

## Cannibalization decisions

1. Review `/blog/d2c-ecommerce-growth/why-is-my-shopify-store-not-converting` versus `/blog/d2c-ecommerce-growth/why-your-shopify-store-is-not-converting`. Same problem intent is apparent from title/URL; ranking harm is not yet established. Compare full content, query-page data and inbound references; preserve the more useful asset and redirect only if redundant.
2. Agency guide: commercial selection checklist lives at `/compare/how-to-choose-a-web-development-agency`; the similarly titled blog must supply a distinct example or be consolidated.
3. Pricing guide owns general cost intent; blog cost articles need actual scenario breakdowns, not duplicate price claims. `/plans` owns Devacia's offered scopes/prices.
4. Ecommerce service sells a build, rescue diagnoses an existing store, CRO sells an improvement process, and an industry page explains the vertical workflow. If content does not sustain these distinctions, combine sections instead of adding more pages.
5. Branded sitelinks across multiple pages are not by themselves cannibalization. Do not force every `devacia` impression onto the homepage.

Opportunity scoring: first assess service capability and gross-margin potential, then immediacy of buyer problem, availability of proof, current visibility and cost to produce a genuinely useful page. Volume is a supporting variable once available. Review the map monthly with qualified pipeline, not merely impression growth.
