## Website scope / quote benchmark pilot

Research question: how do inclusions and ongoing responsibilities vary across comparable website proposals? Do not frame it as a ranking of agencies or a claim that one price buys the same work everywhere.

Prepare three fixed briefs: business website with editable pages and enquiry routing; a specified ecommerce catalogue and checkout; B2B catalogue/RFQ with roles and approvals. State page/product counts, content ownership, integrations, migration needs, support period and currency before collecting responses.

Collect only volunteered/anonymizable proposal evidence with permission, or public published scope pages with a dated source. Do not impersonate a buyer, send unauthorized requests or publish private quotes. Separate public list prices from bespoke proposals. Record nonresponse and exclusions rather than pretending the sample is representative of India.

Record for every observation: ID, collection date, market, source type and URL/private evidence reference, permission status, brief version, platform, scope inclusions/exclusions, one-time fee, tax treatment, recurring software/hosting/support cost, currency, delivery assumptions, content responsibilities, revision policy, maintenance terms and reviewer notes.

Quality checks: two-person review where feasible; deduplicate resellers/identical proposals; compare like scopes; keep missing values missing; do not silently convert tax-inclusive to tax-exclusive. Publish currency-specific summaries unless a dated FX method is justified. State sample size and selection limitations. With an inadequate sample, release the scope checklist rather than a numerical benchmark.

## Fixed AI/search observation panel

Use the exact prompts in `ai-observation-panel.json`. Record date/time, product/model as shown, signed-in status, location, language, browsing setting, prompt, full answer evidence location, named agencies, cited URLs and whether Devacia appears or is cited. Repeat the same settings; log product changes. An unavailable engine is not a zero-visibility result. Separate a brand mention from a link citation.

These prompts are proposed buyer questions, not measured search-volume keywords. Do not aggregate a small panel into a claim about overall AI market share. Publish the actual count and denominator with the observation method.

## 30 / 60 / 90-day and later operating sequence

- First month after release: validate tracking and priority indexing, complete claim permissions, record the first trusted qualification cohort, obtain authenticated keyword metrics, run moderated mobile inquiry tasks.
- Second month: publish a permissioned implementation case and a supporting service answer addressing observed buyer objections; review query overlap before any consolidation; collect the benchmark pilot and AI baseline.
- Third month: release only research that passes the collection and review gates; send authorized relevant pitches; reconcile qualified pipeline and contracted value by acquisition cohort; repeat the fixed AI panel.
- Months 4–6: choose the next market/service from qualified demand, margin and delivery capacity. Add Pune/South India or international content only with a distinct buyer need and usable proof. Improve the existing tools using actual feedback.
- Months 7–12: repeat the same research method, update dated sources, review client evidence and publish the annual comparison only after enough observations exist. Consolidate redundant pages using query and conversion evidence.

No recurring task was scheduled. This is the operating sequence for the owner and team to run after deployment and account validation.
